The GSM 03.38 default alphabet and its single extension table.

Source: 3GPP TS 23.038 §6.2.1 (default alphabet) and Table 6.2.1.1 (the
escape-to-extension table). Both tables are transcribed here verbatim rather
than derived, because every "obvious" derivation of them is wrong somewhere:
`$` is at 0x02 and 0x24 is `¤`; `@` is at 0x00 and 0x40 is `¡`; 0x60 is `¿`,
which is why the ASCII backtick is the one printable ASCII character with no
GSM-7 representation at all.
